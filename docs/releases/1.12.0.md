# MFG Unlock 1.12.0 — DLSS 5 neural rendering

MFG Unlock can now run NVIDIA's DLSS 5 neural rendering (NR) on the game's own frames, in D3D12 and Vulkan games, alongside frame generation and HDR. It has its own tab in the panel and is off by default.

MFG Unlock does not include or download NVIDIA's runtime: put your own `nvngx_dlssnr.dll` beside the game's exe. Nothing else is needed. The add-on carries the small helper it needs and writes it to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MFGUnlock`.

## DLSS 5 tab

Turn on **DLSS 5 neural rendering**, then restart the game once: NR has to be on when the game starts, so that MFG Unlock can place it before the game's frame generation, and in Vulkan games add what NR needs to the game's device.

The status line at the top says where NR runs and what it costs:

- **After the game's upscaler**, with the game's motion vectors and depth (games with DLSS Super Resolution or Ray Reconstruction). NR runs before the HUD and the game's screen effects. This is the best place.
- **Before the game's frame generation**, with its motion vectors and depth (games with DLSS frame generation, or XeMFG DLSS-G, but no DLSS SR). NR runs once per real frame, before frames are generated.
- **Before Present** in any other D3D12 game, with motion that MFG Unlock estimates from the image, or optionally with the game's own TAA motion vectors (**TAA finder**, experimental, off by default).
- **Before Present (Vulkan)** in Vulkan games.

Settings:

- **Look:** network, style, intensity, local tone, structure and skin; built-in looks (Default, Natural, Cinematic, Subtle, Original network) and your own, saved by name.
- **Quality and performance:** resolution (100% by default; 75% or 50% cost much less), **NR passes** (1 to 3: NR again on its own result, for a stronger effect, at about the cost of one more pass each), where NR runs, motion for NR, stability (against flicker where NR has no motion information) and HUD protection.
- **HDR:** HDR10 and scRGB images are handled; the paper white follows Windows' SDR content brightness unless set.
- **Advanced:** hotkeys, the VRAM reserve (NR pauses and frees its memory when video memory runs short, and comes back by itself), and what to do when another DLSS 5 add-on is loaded (MFG Unlock's NR stands aside by default).

Hotkeys: **Ctrl+F8** turns NR on and off, **Ctrl+F7** compares (left half without NR). The performance overlay shows a "DLSS 5 NR x ms" line.

## Games

Tested by us:

- Cyberpunk 2077 and Crimson Desert: after the game's upscaler, with frame generation and HDR.
- The Last of Us Part II: before the game's frame generation, at 4x.
- A Plague Tale: Resonance (Game Pass).
- Resident Evil Requiem: after the game's DLSS. RE Engine games need REFramework with ReShade; with REFramework loaded, NR runs only after the game's DLSS (in gameplay, not in the start menus), because before Present it broke REFramework's overlay when the game resized its swap chain. The game also does not set its compute state again after DLSS, which crashed the driver; MFG Unlock now puts the game's descriptor heaps, root signature and pipeline back after NR, in every game.
- Red Dead Redemption 2 (Vulkan), with renodx-rdr2vk: NR runs. NR's own Vulkan objects go to the driver directly, so other add-ons in ReShade's Vulkan layer do not see or change them.

## Other changes

- **XeMFG DLSS-G in Vulkan** no longer mistakes the game's calls that NR passes on for its own.
- **Panel:** on/off options are switches, and short lists of choices are segmented selectors.
- **Diagnostics:** with `[Diagnostics] ForceDRED=1`, a device loss also writes which command lists the GPU had not finished and the page fault, if any, to the log, and the process' exceptions with their stacks.

## Known limits

- **Red Dead Redemption 2 with renodx-rdr2vk's HDR and Add frame generation:** no frame generation. RenoDX makes its HDR on a copy of the real swap chain's images, while Add frame generation's Streamline gives the game images of its own to draw on, so the two cannot be combined. NR with RenoDX's HDR works, and frame generation works with the game's own HDR. Making both work together would mean placing Streamline below ReShade in Vulkan.
- The game's own frame generation turned on after DLSS 5 (or DLSS 5 turned on after the game started): restart the game.
- Before Present without the game's motion vectors, NR can show more flicker than after the upscaler; raise **Stability**, or use the TAA finder.
- D3D11 games are not supported.
