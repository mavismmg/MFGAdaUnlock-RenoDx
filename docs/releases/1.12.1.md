# MFG Unlock 1.12.1 — DLSS 5: less ghosting, the game's own motion everywhere, XeMFG support

Fixes from the reports on 1.12.0, and DLSS 5 neural rendering (NR) made steadier and cheaper. The games named were tested by their reporters, not by us, unless said otherwise.

## DLSS 5

- **The game's own motion before Present.** In games with their own Streamline (CONTROL, games with DLSS frame generation), "before Present" now uses the motion vectors and depth the game gives its frame generation, instead of motion estimated from the image. In CONTROL at 1440p, NR cost 10.6 ms with the estimate and 5.2 ms with the game's guides, and it flickers less.
- **Less ghosting.**
  - **Camera cuts:** NR starts its history again at the game's camera cut, before its frame generation and before Present; in games that give no cut, MFG Unlock detects it from the image. Before, NR kept the previous scene for a few frames.
  - **HUD:** in games that give an image without the HUD (The Last of Us Part II, CONTROL), NR no longer drags the HUD with the camera. With a still HUD over a moving scene, the HUD changed 23% less between frames in our measurement with NVIDIA's runtime. It follows **Protect HUD**, and stands aside when that image differs from the frame over much of the screen (an effect applied after it, such as RenoDX).
  - **Protect silhouettes (%)** (new, off by default): less of NR's change at the edges of things that move against the scene behind them, such as a character's outline, where NR's history can leave a trail or a halo. Needs the game's motion vectors and depth. Compare with Ctrl+F8 (NR off) to tell NR's trails from frame generation's.
- **One route at a time.** Turning the game's frame generation off and on in a menu no longer moves NR between "before frame generation" and "before Present"; the route in use keeps going until it has not run for 5 s.
- **GPU budget (ms)** (new, off by default): NR lowers its resolution, down to 50%, to stay near this time, and raises it again when there is room.
- **XeMFG:** the DLSS 5 tab is there with the XeMFG method too. NR runs after the game's DLSS Super Resolution: turn on XeMFG's **DLSS SR input** on the General tab and use DLSS SR in the game.
- **The runtime is checked before it loads.** The panel says when `nvngx_dlssnr.dll` is not NVIDIA's NR runtime and which functions it lacks (Once Human), and an unsigned runtime can be allowed with **Allow this file**, without a restart. The log and the support report show its version.
- The status line and the support report show about how much video memory NR uses.
- Styles are named **A**, **B** and **C**.

## Fixes

- **CONTROL Resonant: GPU hang** with DLSS 5 and XeMFG DLSS-G after turning frame generation off and on in the menu. NR restarted on another device object at each change, while the game's frames that used it could still be on the GPU. It now restarts at most once every 5 seconds, after the GPU is done with them (and, with the route change above, rarely at all).
- **The Last of Us Part II:** with the game's frame generation, the DLSS 5 tab said "restart the game" while NR ran (a regression in the test builds, not in 1.12.0).
- **Add frame generation in Kingdom Come: Deliverance II:** GITC Uplift, loaded even with its effect off, created the game's swap chain before MFG Unlock's Streamline could, so no frames were generated. The log, the support report and the General tab now say when the game's swap chain went past Streamline and name the module. Remove it from the game folder.
- **XeMFG panel, compact layout:** choosing DLSS 5 or Diagnostics in the page list opened the wrong page.
- The panel's DLSS 5 notice about `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MFGUnlock` lost its backslash.

## Tested

- Automated: the full test gate (235 tests), and NVIDIA's NR runtime in a test app (the motion vector convention, a still HUD over a moving scene, the control mask).

## Known limits

- The game's own frame generation turned on after DLSS 5 (or DLSS 5 turned on after the game started): restart the game.
- Kingdom Come: Deliverance II with XeMFG and RenoDX's HDR: artifacts in the clouds while the camera moves; nothing in the logs explains it yet.
- D3D11 games are not supported.
